## 30-Second Executive Summary

**upay Sentinel** is an enterprise-grade AI Fraud & Scam Intelligence platform built specifically for Mobile Financial Services (MFS) in Bangladesh. 

Rather than relying on naive black-box LLM classifiers or rigid static rule thresholds, **upay Sentinel** pairs a **sub-millisecond deterministic risk engine** (70% weight) with a **multi-model Python ML ensemble** (30% weight) running atop an **Express + Supabase** backend. It equips risk analysts with:
1. **Authentic 64-District Vector Atlas** with live TPS, volume, and district risk scoring.
2. **4-Stage Sequential Money Trail** tracing funds from victim compromise to underground liquidation.
3. **Google Gemini Forensic Copilot** generating structured explanations and actionable next steps.
4. **Cryptographic SHA-256 Chained Audit Trail** ensuring zero tampering and regulatory compliance with BFIU standards.
5. **Observed IP & Anti-Spoofing Telemetry** mapping login IPs to Bangladeshi telecom ASNs (Grameenphone, Banglalink, Robi) with strict scientific privacy standards.
